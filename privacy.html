Our primary contact for privacy matters: privacy@valatir.ai

3. Local-First Architecture

Valatir stores all application data — conversations, files you upload, workspace state, your Brain Map knowledge graph, settings, and session history — in a SQLite database on your machine. This database is never transmitted to our servers.

What "local-first" means in practice:

  • You can use Valatir without an internet connection (for workspaces that don't require external AI calls)
  • Deleting the application deletes all your data. We have no backup of it.
  • Your data is not used to train AI models (it never reaches us to begin with)
  • You can inspect the SQLite database directly — it is not encrypted by default, though an encryption option is planned for a future release

The one exception: if you use the Managed API Keys add-on, your messages are routed through Valatir's infrastructure to the relevant AI provider. In that case, message content passes through our systems transiently (not stored) to reach the provider. If you use your own API keys, messages go directly from your machine to the provider.

4. Third-Party AI Providers

When you send a message or file to an AI model in Valatir — Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini, Groq, or any other — that content is transmitted to the relevant provider and processed under their privacy policy. Valatir is a client that calls their API; we do not control how they handle your data.

Links to their privacy policies:

  • Anthropic (Claude)
  • OpenAI (GPT-4o)
  • Google (Gemini)
  • Groq

Most API providers do not use API-submitted content to train their models (as distinct from their consumer products). We recommend checking each provider's current API data policy if this matters to you.

5. Waitlist and Email Communications

When you join the waitlist at valatir.com, we collect your email address. We use it to:

  • Send you a confirmation that you're on the list
  • Notify you when early access opens (August 1, 2026)
  • Send product updates and launch announcements (infrequently)

We do not sell your email address. We do not share it with advertisers. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send. To request deletion: privacy@valatir.ai — we will remove your email within 5 business days.

6. Billing and Payments

All billing is handled by LemonSqueezy, who acts as the merchant of record for Valatir subscriptions. LemonSqueezy processes your payment, issues your receipt, and handles tax collection. We do not receive or store your credit card number or bank details.

LemonSqueezy collects and remits GST/HST (Canada), US sales tax, and EU VAT as applicable. Their privacy policy is at lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

7. The Live Build Log (valatir.com/build)

The build progress page at valatir.com/build shows publicly visible data about the development of the Valatir platform: number of files written, tests passing, build cost, and sprint progress. This data is pushed from the Valkyrie build agent to a public GitHub repository and served via Cloudflare.

This data contains no personal information about any user. It is entirely about the software being built, not the people using it.

8. Cookies and Analytics

Valatir.com does not use tracking cookies or behavioural analytics at launch. We may use:

  • Functional cookies — Cloudflare may set cookies for security and performance purposes. These are strictly necessary and not used for advertising.
  • Waitlist state — A local browser storage value to remember if you've already joined the waitlist. Not transmitted anywhere.

If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy and use a privacy-first provider (Plausible or Fathom — no fingerprinting, no personal data, no cross-site tracking).

9. Your Rights

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Ontario's privacy legislation, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (right to erasure)
  • Withdraw consent for email communications at any time
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

To exercise any of these rights: privacy@valatir.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children

Valatir is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@valatir.ai and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes — ones that affect what data we collect or how we use it — will be communicated by email to all registered users at least 14 days before they take effect. Minor changes (clarifications, corrections) will be updated silently with a revised "last updated" date.

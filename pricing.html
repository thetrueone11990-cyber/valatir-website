💬 Common questions

Honest answers.

Why not just use ChatGPT Plus for $20? +
ChatGPT Plus gives you one model (GPT-4o), one interface, and no integration between tools. The moment you need image generation, code execution, web search, and document analysis in the same workflow, you're opening three or four separate tabs — and paying for each of them. Valatir puts all of that in one application, with 200+ models to route tasks to the one that actually handles it best. For most professionals, the math works out at CA$29 vs CA$150–$200/month in fragmented subscriptions.
Is my data sent to the cloud? +
Valatir is local-first. All your files, conversation history, and workspace state are stored in a local SQLite database on your machine — not on our servers. The only data that leaves your computer are the messages and files you explicitly send to an AI provider (Claude, OpenAI, etc.) as part of a request. We never store your conversations server-side. You can review our Privacy Policy for the full picture.
Do I need to sign up for Anthropic and OpenAI separately? +
Not unless you want to. The Managed API Keys add-on (from CA$9/month on Pro+) handles all provider access in one place. If you prefer to bring your own API keys and pay providers directly, that's fully supported too — just paste them into Valatir's settings. Either way works.
What's the difference between Pro and Studio? +
Pro is the complete AI platform: all models, all core workspaces (chat, code, brain graph, image editor, documents, web research, automation), and unlimited everything. Studio adds the media production stack: the DAW workspace for audio generation and editing, video generation, voice synthesis via ElevenLabs, music generation via Suno, and 3D model preview. If you're not a content creator or audio/video professional, Pro is almost certainly the right tier.
Can I cancel any time? +
Yes. Cancel any time — monthly plans stop at the end of your billing cycle, annual plans refund the remaining months pro-rated. Billing is handled by LemonSqueezy, a trusted merchant of record that processes payments for thousands of software companies.
What does "200+ models" actually mean? +
Valatir routes requests through an intelligent multi-provider system. The 200+ figure covers models from Anthropic (Claude family), OpenAI (GPT-4o family), Google (Gemini family), Meta (Llama 3 family via Groq and others), Mistral, Cohere, and a growing list of OpenRouter-compatible models. You can set routing rules — e.g., "use Claude for reasoning tasks, Groq Llama for quick drafts, Gemini Flash for image analysis" — or let the platform choose automatically based on task type.
Is there a student or nonprofit discount? +
Not at launch, but we're planning a verified student/nonprofit programme for Q2 2027. In the meantime, the Free tier covers basic usage and the early access waitlist includes 3 months of Pro free for early adopters — join the list below.
How does annual billing work? +
Annual plans are billed once per year at 10 months' cost (2 months free — approximately 17% off). You can switch between monthly and annual at any time. If you upgrade from monthly to annual mid-cycle, we'll credit your remaining monthly days against the annual price.
